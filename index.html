Amplitude amplification

Highlights · Grover’s Algorithm · p.2

In the n-qubit system, every state can be represented as a unit vector |Ψ⟩ in a 2ⁿ-dimensional Hilbert space. However, Grover’s algorithm operates within a simple geometric structure spanned by |w⟩ and |w⊥⟩, so the whole algorithm reduces to a sequence of planar rotations, despite the underlying space having dimension 2ⁿ.

Grover’s algorithm evolves entirely within the two-dimensional subspace spanned by the marked state |w⟩ and its orthogonal complement |w⊥⟩. Because both the oracle and diffusion operator preserve this subspace, the dynamics reduce to simple planar rotations.
